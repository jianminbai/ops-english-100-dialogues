A / Ops Engineer: After approval, I will apply the exception for two hours and compare blocked requests, checkout errors, and completed orders. We will review at 12:30 UTC.

B / Application Lead: I will own functional validation and provide results within fifteen minutes of the change. Support will confirm whether the reported customer cases can complete checkout.
