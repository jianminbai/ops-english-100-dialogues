SCENARIO 049
第三方接口故障

B / Business Lead: Is this our issue or the payment provider's issue? Support needs a clear explanation because customers are reporting failed payment attempts.

A / Ops Engineer: We see failures on the provider path, but we're validating both sides before assigning the cause. Their public status page still reports normal service.

C / Vendor Manager: What evidence do you have so far? I can open a priority support case, but the provider will ask for specific examples.

A / Ops Engineer: Twelve percent of authorization requests returned gateway errors over fifteen minutes. Our request format, credentials, and network reachability checks passed for the sampled calls.

B / Business Lead: Does that mean those customers weren't charged? We need to avoid telling them to retry if the first attempt might still complete.

A / Ops Engineer: Not necessarily. Some responses are ambiguous, so we'll check transaction status using the provider's documented process and reconcile against our order records.

C / Vendor Manager: Please send sanitized request IDs, UTC timestamps, response codes, and the completed checks. I'll use the approved support channel and exclude payment details.

A / Ops Engineer: I'll include examples from successful and failed calls, plus the affected region. That comparison may help them distinguish routing trouble from account configuration.

B / Business Lead: Can we route customers to our other payment method while the case is open? It's already available, but we need to understand its capacity.

A / Ops Engineer: I'll ask its service owner to assess headroom and confirm the approved customer flow. We shouldn't switch pending transactions without checking their current status.

C / Vendor Manager: I'll own the provider escalation and request an acknowledgement. I'll update the incident in fifteen minutes even if they haven't confirmed a cause or recovery estimate.

B / Business Lead: I'll coordinate the customer message: some payments are failing, pending transactions are being checked, and the next update will follow that checkpoint.
