SCENARIO 020
容量与成本评审

A / Ops Engineer: At the current growth rate, we expect to reach our planning threshold in six weeks. That threshold preserves reserve capacity rather than marking total exhaustion.

B / Finance Partner: Does that mean we need to double the infrastructure budget now? I need to understand the spending request before next month’s forecast is finalized.

A / Ops Engineer: Not necessarily. We will compare right-sizing, moving flexible batch work away from peak periods, and adding capacity incrementally where the measured bottlenecks justify it.

C / Infrastructure Lead: What assumptions support the six-week estimate? Average utilization can hide peak pressure, and any proposal must preserve service capacity when one node is unavailable.

A / Ops Engineer: The estimate uses eight weeks of peak usage and the announced customer rollout. I will show a baseline forecast and a higher-demand case, including node failure.

B / Finance Partner: Please separate confirmed demand from assumptions. I would also like to see one-time implementation costs, ongoing charges, and the confidence range for any projected savings.

C / Infrastructure Lead: Before reducing server sizes, check workload variability and recovery requirements. A machine that looks idle on average may provide needed capacity during a failure.

A / Ops Engineer: Agreed. We will identify candidates from workload history and test changes individually. We will also check database limits so extra application instances do not shift the bottleneck.

B / Finance Partner: Could the proposal include a staged spending option? Releasing funds against measured demand would be easier to approve than buying the entire forecast upfront.

A / Ops Engineer: Yes. I will define capacity triggers, expected lead times, and owners for each stage. We must order early enough to avoid consuming the required reserve.

C / Infrastructure Lead: I will validate technical feasibility and resilience assumptions by November 17 at 16:00 UTC. Please bring comparable options with the same demand and availability targets.

B / Finance Partner: I will review the cost ranges on November 18 at 16:00 UTC. We can choose an initial stage then, with later spending conditional on the agreed capacity triggers.
