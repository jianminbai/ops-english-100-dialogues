礼貌催办逾期任务

A / Ops Engineer: The alerting action was due yesterday, November 10. Could you share the current status and any blockers so we can keep the review record accurate?

B / Regional Engineer: The rule change is ready, but the test environment is unavailable because another team extended its maintenance. I haven't run the required failure replay.

A / Ops Engineer: Thanks for explaining. Is the remaining work limited to validation, or are there implementation changes that might still affect the revised delivery date?

B / Regional Engineer: Validation is the known remaining step. If replay exposes a problem, we'll need another revision, so I can't honestly promise completion before testing.

A / Ops Engineer: What support would unblock the test, and who can help secure an environment? We should avoid silently moving the due date without recording the dependency.

B / Regional Engineer: I need the platform coordinator to confirm a slot. I'll contact them today and provide a definite scheduling update by November 12 at 12:00 UTC.

A / Ops Engineer: Could another approved test environment provide equivalent coverage, or does this replay depend on a configuration available only in the unavailable environment?

B / Regional Engineer: A shared environment might work, but we'd need isolation and realistic queue settings. I will ask the platform coordinator to assess it rather than assume equivalence.

A / Ops Engineer: Until validation is complete, the original detection gap remains. Is the interim monitoring coverage still active, and does the on-call team understand its limitations?

B / Regional Engineer: Yes. The manual check is assigned during each shift, and its limitations are documented. I'll reconfirm that coverage in today's regional handover.

A / Ops Engineer: I'll retain the original due date and mark the action blocked, with your scheduling checkpoint. Please escalate early if the platform team cannot offer an option.

B / Regional Engineer: Understood. My November 12 update will include the confirmed test slot or the unresolved dependency, plus a revised completion estimate supported by that information.
