SCENARIO 008
说明任务延期

A / Ops Engineer: The upgrade is behind schedule because the compatibility test failed in staging. We cannot complete the production readiness review with the current evidence.

B / Operations Manager: What failed, and how does it affect the production date? We have already reserved the maintenance window and informed several support teams.

A / Ops Engineer: The reporting agent cannot connect after the library update. Core application checks passed, but reporting is a required function for the business owner.

B / Operations Manager: Could we proceed without that agent and repair it afterward, or would that create an unacceptable gap in the service?

A / Ops Engineer: The owner has not accepted that gap, and the current change approval includes working reports. We need another test cycle or an approved scope change.

B / Operations Manager: Understood. Do you have a confirmed fix, or are we still estimating how much investigation remains? I need to explain the uncertainty clearly.

A / Ops Engineer: The vendor suggested a compatible agent version, but we have not validated it. The application team will test installation, report generation, and rollback tomorrow.

B / Operations Manager: What support would help that testing finish efficiently? Please identify a specific dependency instead of simply asking everyone to prioritize the upgrade.

A / Ops Engineer: We need a reporting specialist for two hours and access to representative test data. I have requested both, but the specialist is not confirmed yet.

B / Operations Manager: I will contact the reporting manager today. Please publish the blocker, its owner, and the validation criteria so the teams understand what must change.

A / Ops Engineer: I will update the project record today and hold the next checkpoint tomorrow at 15:00 UTC. After validation, I will propose a revised production date.

B / Operations Manager: Agreed. I will tell stakeholders the current window is at risk and confirm its status at that checkpoint, without promising an unvalidated completion date.
